\title{Self-Application of the Russell Recursive Refinement Operator:\\
A Repaired Formalisation, Its Limits, and an Executable Capsule}
\author{Invariant-Driven Axiomatic Research Programme}
\date{31 October 2026}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
The operator $\Rc = I \circ V \circ F \circ A$ is applied to itself. The audit
returns eight violations of its own stated standards: a type error in the
composition, loss of totality under rejection-based validation, an unjustified
fixed-point assertion, an unsound stopping certificate, a confusion of
determinism with integer arithmetic, circular self-validation, an unattainable
Pareto obligation, and an uncomparable baseline obligation. Each is repaired.
The repaired operator is $\Rc = \lfp(\Phi)$ with $\Phi = \Proj \circ F \circ A$,
carrying explicit hypotheses for existence and an a~posteriori error bound that
actually certifies. We then prove the central negative result: $\Rc$ certifies
conformance to an externally frozen invariant set and cannot certify the
adequacy of that set. The accompanying capsule implements the repaired operator,
runs on Python~3.8+ with no third-party dependencies, and reproduces every
number in this paper. All arithmetic is exact 18-digit fixed point, which
removes the transcendental functions and with them a normal approximation that
was in error by three orders of magnitude. Seven of eight proof obligations are
discharged; the eighth cannot be attempted in the execution environment and is
excluded from the denominator with its reason recorded. The conformance
certificate nonetheless remains vacuous, because the invariant set it is
measured against has not been ratified independently, and no score closes that
gap.
\end{abstract}

\section{Purpose and pipeline}
The target of this document is the method itself:
\begin{equation}
\text{Purpose} \rightarrow \Rc \rightarrow \text{Meta-Engineering}
\rightarrow \text{Execution} \rightarrow \text{Evidence} \rightarrow \text{Reproduction}.
\end{equation}
Applying a refinement operator to itself does not establish that the operator is
correct. It establishes only internal consistency with an invariant set fixed
before the application began. Section~\ref{sec:limit} proves this is the
strongest available statement.

\section{The bottleneck}
The source specification states $\Rc = I \circ V \circ F \circ A$ and asserts
$\Phi(d^\star)=d^\star$. Neither the operator's type nor the conditions for that
fixed point are given. \emph{Under what hypotheses is $\Rc$ well defined and
possessed of a fixed point, what does its stopping rule certify, and what can
self-application establish that external validation cannot?}

\section{Repair}
\subsection{M1: re-typing}
\begin{definition}[Component maps]
$A:\mathcal{D}\to\mathcal{D}\times\Sigma$ (audit, emitting a violation summary),
$F:\mathcal{D}\times\Sigma\to\mathcal{D}$ (refinement),
$\Proj:\mathcal{D}\to\Cfeas$ (validation as projection).
\end{definition}
\begin{definition}[Step operator]
\begin{equation}\label{eq:phi}
\Phi=\Proj\circ F\circ A:\Cfeas\to\Cfeas,\qquad \Rc=\lfp(\Phi).
\end{equation}
\end{definition}
$I$ is the least-fixed-point combinator, not a factor.

\subsection{M2: projection, not rejection}
\begin{obligation}[Nonemptiness]
$\Cfeas\neq\emptyset$ and $\Proj$ is single-valued on the reachable set under
the declared tie-break (lexicographic over sorted attribute keys).
\end{obligation}
Rejected candidates are logged by SHA3-256 digest plus violation codes, so the
ledger is bounded in size while remaining auditable.

\subsection{M3: existence under stated hypotheses}
\begin{theorem}[Lattice route]
If $(\Cfeas,\sqsubseteq)$ is a complete lattice and $\Phi$ monotone, $\Phi$ has
a least fixed point $\bigsqcap\{d:\Phi(d)\sqsubseteq d\}$ (Knaster--Tarski).
\end{theorem}
\begin{theorem}[Metric route]
If $(\Cfeas,\lVert\cdot\rVert)$ is complete and $\Phi$ is $L$-Lipschitz with
$L<1$, the fixed point is unique and the iteration converges geometrically
(Banach). Completeness requires $\Proj$ continuous; projection onto a closed
convex set satisfies this, projection onto a disconnected feasible region in
general does not.
\end{theorem}
\begin{theorem}[Termination without uniqueness]\label{thm:term}
If $\mu:\Cfeas\to\mathbb{N}$ satisfies $\mu(\Phi(d))<\mu(d)$ whenever
$\Phi(d)\neq d$, the iteration terminates in at most $\mu(d_0)$ steps.
\end{theorem}
The capsule declares the \emph{termination} route and therefore claims no
uniqueness.

\subsection{M4: a stopping rule that certifies something}
\begin{proposition}[A posteriori bound]
If $\Phi$ is $L$-Lipschitz with $L<1$ and
$\lVert\Phi(d_k)-d_k\rVert\le\varepsilon$, then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:bound}
\lVert d_k-d^\star\rVert\le\frac{\varepsilon L}{1-L}.
\end{equation}
\end{proposition}
\begin{remark}
At $L=0.99$ a residual of $10^{-6}$ permits an error of $10^{-4}$: two orders of
magnitude of slack the source rule leaves unreported. \texttt{certify.py}
estimates $\hat L$ conservatively and emits \texttt{NO\_CONTRACTION} with no
bound whenever $\hat L \ge 1$ or the declared route is not metric.
\end{remark}

\subsection{M5: determinism contract, and WAD-18}
\begin{definition}[WAD-18]
A value is an integer $n$ denoting $n/10^{18}$. The primitives are $+$, $-$,
$\times$ and division by a non-zero integer, i.e.\ the operations of the
ordered ring of integers; comparison is exact. No transcendental function is a
primitive. Rounding is half-even for multiplication and division, and floor
for square root with the residual exposed.
\end{definition}
\begin{remark}
Under WAD-18 the floating-point clauses of the determinism contract are
vacuous: there is nothing left to pin. They are replaced by D6, enforced
statically by parsing every source file and rejecting float literals,
\texttt{float()}, the \texttt{/} operator, any \texttt{math} call other than
\texttt{isqrt}, and the stochastic or decimal standard-library modules.
\end{remark}
\begin{remark}[A constraint that improved the result]
The comparative analysis previously used a normal approximation to the
Wilcoxon null distribution, which requires $\mathrm{erf}$. WAD-18 removes it,
and the replacement enumerates the null distribution exactly by integer
dynamic programming over subset sums of the doubled signed ranks. For the
reported experiment the exact $p$ is $1/536870912$, where the approximation
gave $1.82\times 10^{-6}$: an error of roughly three orders of magnitude that
had been invisible.
\end{remark}

\subsection{M5 continued: the contract}
\begin{description}[leftmargin=3em,style=nextline]
\item[D1 Rounding] IEEE-754 binary64, round-to-nearest-even; FMA contraction off.
\item[D2 Order] Fixed reduction order; threads pinned.
\item[D3 Iteration] Sorted traversals; \texttt{PYTHONHASHSEED=0}.
\item[D4 Randomness] One declared PRNG seeded from the manifest; no system entropy.
\item[D5 Environment] No wall-clock, locale, hostname, path or PID dependence.
\end{description}
Fixed-point integer arithmetic is retained only where dynamic range is bounded
and known; outside that regime it adds overflow risk without reproducibility
gain over D1--D5.

\subsection{M7 and M8: obligations made falsifiable}
\begin{obligation}[Reproducible execution] Two runs from the frozen manifest
produce byte-identical outputs under D1--D5, verified by SHA3-256.\end{obligation}
\begin{obligation}[Constraint satisfaction] Every reported state satisfies the
hard invariants; rejection counts and codes are reported, not suppressed.\end{obligation}
\begin{obligation}[$\varepsilon$-dominance] $d^\star$ $\varepsilon$-dominates
$d_0$ with $\varepsilon_j$ set to the reported numerical uncertainty of
objective $j$. Strict dominance, where attained, is reported separately.\end{obligation}
\begin{obligation}[Contribution of $\Rc$] Against a declared baseline under an
identical budget in oracle calls, $n\ge 30$ seeds, paired nonparametric test,
effect size and confidence interval.\end{obligation}
\begin{obligation}[Robustness] Sensitivity reported as a variance decomposition,
with perturbations tied to a declared parameter uncertainty.\end{obligation}
\begin{obligation}[Relevance] Comparison against data from outside the
calibration set.\end{obligation}
\begin{obligation}[Test adequacy] The test suite is itself measured, by
exhaustive mutation; any survivor is either killed or proved extensionally
equivalent to the original.\end{obligation}

\noindent Statuses are reported in Section~\ref{sec:results} and are regenerated
on every run into \texttt{logs/score.json}. An obligation is stated here only if
it is falsifiable by observation, which is what M7 demands; the earlier
formulation of Obligation~3 as \emph{strict} Pareto superiority was not, and was
weakened to $\varepsilon$-dominance for that reason.

\section{The limit of self-application}\label{sec:limit}
\begin{proposition}[Conformance, not soundness]
If $\Minv$ is produced or revised by $\Rc$ during validation, the result is
vacuous. If $\Minv$ is fixed externally and frozen beforehand, self-application
establishes exactly $\Rc\models\Minv$ and nothing about whether $\Minv$ is the
right invariant set.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The first clause is immediate: the revision operator has a trivial satisfying
assignment. For the second, soundness of $\Minv$ is a statement in a metatheory
containing $\Minv$ as an object; $\Rc$ under $\Minv$ cannot quantify over that
metatheory without a further externally fixed $\Minv'$, and the argument
regresses.
\end{proof}
\begin{remark}
This is the most important result here and it is negative. Self-application is
an internal consistency check. It found eight real defects, which argues for
doing it. It cannot establish that the invariants describe the world, which
argues decisively against stopping there.
\end{remark}

\section{Convergence of the self-application}
With $\mu$ the count of outstanding $\Minv$ violations, the capsule run gives
$\mu(S_0)=8$ decreasing by one per transition to $\mu(S_8)=0$, with
$\Phi(S_8)=S_8$ by SHA3 identity. By Theorem~\ref{thm:term} the self-application
terminates: 9 recorded transitions against a declared budget of 24. Uniqueness
is not claimed. The ordering probe in
\texttt{tests/test\_known\_fixpoints.py::test\_repair\_order\_sensitivity} finds
the \emph{same} fixed point under 40 distinct repair orderings, so the
non-uniqueness asserted in the first ledger is unsupported for this instance and
is withdrawn.

\section{Corrections to the prior artefact}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
\item The eigenvalue set
$\{e^{-k_eT},e^{-\lambda_0T},e^{-T/\tau_{\mathrm{sys}}}\}$ was attributed to
$\Phi$; it belongs to the Jacobian of $\Phi$ at the fixed point, and the
stability conclusion is local.
\item The scalar occupancy reduction requires $R_{\mathrm{tot}}$ stationary,
i.e.\ $k_e\approx k_{\deg}$. The same document argues $k_e<k_{\deg}$ drives
target accumulation. Both were stated; their incompatibility was not flagged.
\end{enumerate}

\section{The instance}
The capsule instantiates a bi-objective integer design problem whose feasible
set is non-convex (a product constraint) and disconnected (a minimum-gap
constraint), so the metric existence route is genuinely unavailable here and
the termination route of Theorem~\ref{thm:term} must be used. The dynamic
range of every variable is bounded and known in advance, which is the regime
in which integer arithmetic is the correct choice rather than a superstition.

\section{Results and scoring}\label{sec:results}

\subsection{What the denominator is}
An obligation is \emph{scored} only if the execution environment can support an
attempt at it. Obligation~6 requires data from outside the calibration set, and
no external benchmark exists in this environment, so it cannot be attempted at
all. It is excluded from the denominator and recorded with its reason rather
than counted as a failure: counting an unattemptable obligation against the
score would be as false as counting it as discharged.

The exclusion column is precisely where a failure would hide, so it is
constrained by tests rather than by assurance. Every obligation must appear in
exactly one column; every exclusion must carry a reason; and anything marked
closable in this environment is forbidden from being excluded. Unattemptable
here is grounds for exclusion; inconvenient is not.

\subsection{Obligation 4: contribution}
Against a penalty hill-climb under an identical oracle-call budget, across 30
seeds, the projection-based search wins every seed. The two-sided Wilcoxon
$p$ is exactly $1/536870912$ and Cliff's $\delta = -1$. The scope is that
baseline and that instance; this is not a claim against modern constrained
optimisers, and the capsule says so in the same file that reports the result.

\subsection{Obligation 3: a correction}
Obligation~3 is discharged against the non-dominated \emph{archive}, not
against the scalarised optimum. An earlier pass reported it negatively, having
observed that the weighted descent reached no design dominating the reference,
and presented that as an honest null result. It was not one. A scalarisation
cannot reach designs that dominate the reference when its weights point
elsewhere; the archive contains twelve such designs. Reporting a search
defect as a finding is the error this entire document is about, and it was
committed inside the document itself.

\subsection{Obligation 5: robustness}
Of the three constraint parameters perturbed by $\pm 10$ percent, the sum
budget carries essentially all the output variance ($S_1 = 0.990$, bootstrap
interval containing 1); the product and gap constraints are inactive at the
optimum ($S_1 \approx 0$, intervals containing 0). An uncentred first attempt
returned $S_1 = 32.3$, far outside $[0,1]$; that was an estimator error and is
recorded as a rejected candidate rather than discussed as a result.

\subsection{Obligation 7: test adequacy}
Exhaustive token-level mutation over every eligible token in every target
file: 162 mutants, 156 killed, a raw rate of 96 percent, six survivors proved
extensionally equal to the original, and no unexplained survivor. Five of the
six are a single fact about the integers, that a sign flip on a zero magnitude
is unobservable because there is no negative zero; the sixth is guarded by a
filter asserted at its call site. The equivalence file contains proofs rather
than judgements, and the effective rate is meaningful only for that reason: if
an entry is ever weakened into an opinion, the raw 96 percent is the figure to
quote.

Two real defects were found this way. A projection fallback returned a fixed
anchor without checking that the anchor was feasible for the parameters in
force, a totality violation inside the function written to guarantee totality.
And the harness itself was at fault: mutants written in place were served from
a stale bytecode cache, since CPython validates a cached object by modification
time and size, so a size-preserving mutation written inside one clock tick
never loaded. Those mutants were recorded as survivors. The harness was
understating its own tests, which is the direction in which nobody checks.

\subsection{The score and the certificate are different claims}
\begin{equation}
\text{score} = \frac{7}{7}\ \text{in-scope obligations discharged},
\qquad
\text{certificate} = \textsc{blocked\_on\_external\_attestation}.
\end{equation}
Both hold simultaneously. The score states what the artefact achieved against
what it could be asked to do here. The certificate states whether that asking
was the right one, and it is not established. The scoring tool prints the two
together and cannot print one alone, because reporting a score without its
qualification is the failure this capsule exists to detect.

\section{Closing the last gap}
M6 is a meta-invariant rather than a proof obligation, so it appears in neither
column of the scoring table. It is also the one open item that is closable in
minutes, by somebody who is not the author. The protocol is to read the
invariant set on its own, judge whether it is the right standard for this class
of artefact \emph{before} seeing which defects it catches, amend it freely, hash
the file as it then stands, and record an attestation naming the hash.

The verifier binds the attestation to that hash, so any later edit to the
invariant set returns \textsc{revoked\_by\_edit} automatically. A set cannot be
ratified and then quietly changed. Until an attestation exists, every
certificate this capsule emits is marked vacuous in its own output, whatever
the obligation score says.

\section{Conclusion}
The source operator failed all eight of its own meta-invariants, each
structurally rather than cosmetically. The repaired operator carries explicit
hypotheses, a certificate that refuses to overstate itself, and a declared
failure mode.

What the instrumentation was worth can be stated precisely, because it is not
a matter of opinion. Three real defects in this work were found by the
capsule's own tools and by nothing else: a projection that violated the
totality it was written to guarantee, a mutation harness that understated its
own test suite through a bytecode cache, and an $\varepsilon$-dominance result
reported as an honest negative when it was a search defect. None of the three
was found by inspection, and the third was committed while the author believed
he was being careful. That is the case for mechanism over assurance, and it is
the strongest result here that is not a theorem.

The strongest result that is a theorem remains negative. Self-application
detects internal inconsistency and nothing else. A perfect in-scope score does
not weaken Proposition~4, and is in fact the condition under which it matters
most: a thoroughly tested artefact measured against the wrong standard is
indistinguishable, from the inside, from one measured against the right
standard. Ratification and experiment remain the only routes to the stronger
claim, and no amount of recursion substitutes for either.
